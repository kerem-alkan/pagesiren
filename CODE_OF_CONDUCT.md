# Code of conduct

PageSiren is a small project, and people come to it from all over the world. Let's keep it a friendly place.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to this.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or attacks on anyone for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Threats, sexual content, or anything else that makes people not want to be here.
- Spam.

## If something goes wrong

Write to [contact@keremalkan.com](mailto:contact@keremalkan.com). Reports stay private. Comments that break these rules may be removed, and people who keep breaking them may be blocked.

These rules apply in this repository's issues, pull requests and other project spaces. They are inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
